**Tomb Raider: Coda di Volpe** – beta 1. A fan adventure in four levels set in Oliveri, Marinello and Tindari (Sicily), made with TombEngine 2.0.

## How to play
Download the zip below, unpack it anywhere and start `PLAY.exe` (Windows 10/11, 64 bit). Nothing to install. The opening video can be skipped with Look or Deselect.

## Levels
1. Oliveri – Via Amodeo
2. Marinello – The witch of the lagoons
3. Tindari
4. The lost half
