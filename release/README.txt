TOMB RAIDER - CODA DI VOLPE
A fan adventure in four levels, set in Oliveri, Marinello and Tindari (Sicily).

HOW TO PLAY
Start PLAY.exe. Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. Nothing to install: keep the Engine folder next to PLAY.exe.
The opening video can be skipped with Look or Deselect.

LEVELS
1. Oliveri - Via Amodeo
2. Marinello - The witch of the lagoons
3. Tindari
4. The lost half
