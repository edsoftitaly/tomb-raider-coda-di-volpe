STORY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Lara is spending a quiet holiday in Oliveri, a small seaside town on
the north coast of Sicily, beneath the cape of Tindari. She has brought
with her an old Byzantine diary that speaks of the Stars of the
Dioscuri: two relics, Castor on land and Pollux under the sea, that
were said to command the tides of the bay.

One night thieves break into the house. They work for Turi "'a Vulpi"
(the Fox), a grave robber and former tuna fisherman who wants the two
Stars to sell them abroad. They take the diary, and Lara follows the
Fox's trail from the old town to the castle, along the seafront at
dawn, into the cave of Donna Villa - the sorceress of the local legend -
across the lagoons of Marinello and up to the Greek city of Tyndaris.

Below the cape, under the sea, the drowned half of Tyndaris is waiting.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVELS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Oliveri - night in the old town, the house in Via Gaspare Amodeo,
   the Saia canal and the castle.
2. Il Lungomare - the seafront at dawn, on the gang's motorbike.
3. Marinello - the railway keeper's house and the cave of Donna Villa.
4. I Laghetti - the lagoons, the sunken tuna trap and the sandbar.
5. Tindari - the sanctuary, the Greek theatre, the crypt.
6. La Citta' Sommersa - the drowned city and the temple of the Stars.

12 secrets (the Stars of the Dioscuri) are hidden in the game.

The levels are built at real scale (1 square = 2 metres) on the real
terrain of the area, so the places can be recognised on a map.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Extract the archive to a folder of your choice and run TombEngine.exe.
No other installation is needed.
