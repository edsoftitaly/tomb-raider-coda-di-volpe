# Tomb Raider: Coda di Volpe

A custom Tomb Raider adventure in four levels, set in Oliveri, Marinello and Tindari, on the north coast of Sicily (province of Messina). Built with TombEngine and Tomb Editor.

Conceived, written and directed by **Edoardo Napoli**.

## Download

The game is on the [Releases](../../releases) page. Unzip it and start `PLAY.exe` (Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit). Nothing to install.

## The levels

| # | Level | Place |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oliveri - Via Amodeo | A house in the old village, at night |
| 2 | Marinello - The witch of the lagoons | The sand spit, the lagoons and Donna Villa's cave |
| 3 | Tindari | The Greek and Roman town on the headland, and the sanctuary |
| 4 | The lost half | The half of Tyndaris that fell into the sea |

The story is in [STORY.md](STORY.md). It gives the whole plot away, so play first.
