TOMB RAIDER - CODA DI VOLPE
Walkthrough (release 1.0, 20 November 2026)

This file gives the whole solution. Stop reading if you would rather find things out for yourself.


GENERAL NOTES

- Doors of houses are kicked open: stand facing the door and press Action. Holster your weapons first, or
  nothing happens. The same goes for keys, cranks and anything else Lara does with her hands.
- A key goes in only when Lara stands right in front of its lock, facing it. Press Action there and the key is
  offered; anywhere else nothing happens.
- The pushable crate is the wooden one. Grab it with Action, then Forward or Back.
- Statues, furniture, cars, columns and crates are solid. Reeds and bushes are not.
- The men of the gang drop what they carry when they die: ammunition and medipacks. Search the bodies.
- Every level hides three Stars of the Dioscuri (the secrets, twelve in all). Find all three in a level and the
  Twins leave you medipacks and ammunition.
- Saving is free. A savegame belongs to the version of the level it was made in.


WEAPONS

  Pistols            from the start
  Shotgun            Oliveri (in the house) and Marinello (on the table in the house of the rais)
  Uzis               Oliveri, in the workshop on the ground floor
  Harpoon gun        Marinello, on the shore of the Mergolo
  Crossbow           Marinello, in the gallery that leads out of the witch's cave
  Revolver           Tindari, in the dig, near the camp
  HK                 Tindari, in the cryptoporticus, beyond the iron gate
  Grenade launcher   Tindari, in the Roman quarter
  Rocket launcher    The Lost Half, in the shop by the cistern

Ammunition for each lies in the same level and in the next ones.


LEVEL 1 - PROLOGUE: VIA AMODEO (Oliveri, night)

Goal: get the Byzantine diary back from the thieves.

1. You wake in the bedroom on the top floor. The power is out. Go down the two flights of stairs: there are
   thieves on every floor. The one on the first floor carries the Box Room Key.
2. The front door is barred from outside. The way out is the hatch in the ceiling of the box room, on the top
   floor. Stand in front of the lock beside the box room door and use the key. A thief with a club waits inside:
   he carries the Padlock Key.
3. Before you leave the house, the power: the workshop on the ground floor has an electric lock. Take the Fuse
   from the cellar and put it in the fuse box in the hall (north wall, beside the pantry door): stand in front
   of the box. The lights come back and the workshop door can be kicked open.
4. In the workshop: the Uzis, and a computer with a line on the screen: 33 2D 32 2D 35. Those are character
   codes: 3-2-5. You will need them in the street.
5. Back up to the box room. Stand in front of the padlock, use the Padlock Key, climb the ladder to the roof.
6. On the roof go east over the neighbours' roofs: two running jumps over the gaps. If you fall into a gap you
   can climb back up. At the far end a drainpipe goes down to the street.
7. In the street the lookout runs into the courtyard and shuts the gate; his dogs come for you. Five numbered
   switches on the wall control the gate: put 2, 3 and 5 on, 1 and 4 off.
8. Kill the lookout in the courtyard and take the diary from where he fell. The Carabinieri have closed the
   street: leave by the town hall end.

Stars: one in the cellar; one in the street, at the east end; one up on the roofs, near the drainpipe.


LEVEL 2 - THE WITCH OF THE LAKES (Marinello, dawn)

Goal: reach Donna Villa's cave in the cliff and take the Star of Castor.

1. The old tuna fishery. Kick the doors of the houses open. In the house of the rais: the ledger (the crank is
   in the net store, the two levers of the Seal are on the lake bed) and the Shotgun on the table.
2. The net store: push the wooden crate onto the stone slab with the iron ring. A block rises by the loft:
   climb it. Up there, the Crank (and a man waiting).
3. In the yellow house, the winch of the Mergolo sluice: stand in front of it and use the Crank. The lake
   empties.
4. Walk down onto the bed of the Mergolo. Two iron levers, far apart: pull one and you have ten seconds to
   pull the other. Do it right and the Seal chamber opens up at the fishery. Take the Seal of the Tonnara.
5. The gate to the reed bed has an iron plate with a round recess. Stand in front of the plate and use the Seal.
6. Beyond the reeds the Green Lake is dry mud, and the ledge of the cave is five metres up. The gang's foreman
   is here: he carries the Sluice House Key.
7. The sluice house stands on the stone shoulder above the great sluice. Jump to the iron pole and climb.
   Stand in front of the brass lock of the iron cabinet and use the key: the sea fills the Green Lake.
8. Swim to the witch's ledge. Behind it the sinkhole: no way back from here.
9. In the cave: the hall of the five shells. Press Action at each altar in this order: south-west, north-west,
   south-east, north-east, and last the one that stands alone at the south end. A wrong shell starts the count
   again. Then Donna Villa's pool: take the Star of Castor from the pedestal across the water.
10. The spirits wake. Bullets do nothing to them: lead each one into the water and it dies. When the last is
    gone the way out opens. In the gallery, boulders: use the niches. The Crossbow is in this gallery.
11. Climb the iron rungs of the shaft to Tindari.

Stars: one high in the fishery, on a roof; one under water at the east shore; one under water in the Mergolo.


LEVEL 3 - TINDARI (afternoon)

Goal: follow Turi through the crypt under the sanctuary.

1. The grave robbers' camp. The house in the dig is locked; the keyhole is in the wall beside the door. The
   foreman has the key and he is down in the Greek theatre, to the west.
2. The theatre: the foreman and his men are in the arena. He drops the Dig Site Key. Go back, stand in front
   of the keyhole and use it.
3. Through the Roman house: the mosaic of the caps. The inscription says that Castor, the mortal, treads the
   earth: walk only on the caps the panel gives to Castor. The others give way. In the Roman quarter beyond it:
   the Terrace Key and the Grenade launcher.
4. The basilica of the dig: a stair goes down into the cryptoporticus. The floor has fallen onto iron spikes:
   swing across on the rope. The iron gate at the end takes the Terrace Key; its keyhole is in the pillar
   beside it. Beyond the gate, the HK.
5. Up on the headland, the sanctuary of the Black Madonna. The bronze door is ajar. Inside, behind the high
   altar, under the golden shrine carried by the two angels: the Star of Castor. Under the dome three windows
   are different from the others: a moon, a star, a sun, in that order.
6. The crypt of the twins: two statues that can be pushed and a bronze wheel beside the gate. Put the Star of
   Castor in the socket of the wheel. One day for each of them: Castor on the west pedestal and Pollux on the
   east one, turn the wheel; swap them, turn the wheel; then both side by side on the two middle plates, turn
   the wheel. The gate opens.
7. Down the shaft to the sea caves.

Stars: one at the top of the theatre; one in the dig, near the Roman house; one in the cryptoporticus, in the
alcove beside the iron gate.


LEVEL 4 - THE LOST HALF (the drowned city)

Goal: stop Turi and bring both Stars out.

1. The sea caves, then a Roman street under the sea. The gate at the far end has an empty pedestal either
   side: it wants the two Caps of the Dioscuri.
2. The baths: the pool is dry. When the water rises, go up to the loggia. The Cap of Castor is here.
3. The cistern: a lattice of caps, dark and golden, over deep water. The same rule as at Tindari: Castor's
   caps hold, the others give way. The Cap of
   Pollux is beyond it. The shop beside the cistern holds the Rocket launcher.
4. Set the two caps on the pedestals. The gate opens on the temple of the Dioscuri. The cella lies eight
   metres below, with Turi's men in it.
5. The altar of the Stars has two sockets. With the Star of Castor on the altar the sea fills the cella; take
   it off and the cella drains. Use this to get down and to open the valves: pull the lever in the cella, and
   with the weight of the water gone the bronze doors open.
6. Turi 'a Vulpi wears the Star of Pollux and cannot be killed: he flies, throws fire and brings fire down
   from the vault. Do not waste ammunition on him. Run back to the altar and set the Star of Castor: the sea
   fills the cella and takes him. If you catch fire, it goes out by itself in a moment, or in the water.
7. The Star of Pollux floats up. Set it in the second socket. The vault starts to give way and the spirits
   come: lead them into the water, as in Donna Villa's cave.
8. The bronze door gives. Run: boulders again, use the niches. Out into the dawn over the sand spit of
   Marinello.

Stars: one under water in the siphon of the sea caves; one in the shop by the cistern; one in the cella.
